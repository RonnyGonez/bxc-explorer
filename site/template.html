    Find missing songs and bulk add them to the playlist

    Finds songs shared in the WhatsApp group that aren't in the playlist yet, so you can add them all in one go. Start on your phone, finish on a computer.

    On your phone

    1. In WhatsApp, open the group, tap its name, then Export chat → Without media.
    2. Send the export to yourself, by email or AirDrop, so you can open it on your computer.

    On your computer

    1. Open this page, click the box below and pick the export.
    2. Click Copy all missing links.
    3. In the Spotify app, open the playlist, click in the track list and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac). They all drop in.

    The chat is read in your browser only and never uploaded.

    How this works

    Playing: songs play in the player at the bottom of the screen and move on by themselves. On a computer, sign in at open.spotify.com in the same browser to hear full songs; otherwise Spotify plays 30-second previews. Or tap Play full songs here once and sign in with Spotify: from then on everything plays in full, right in this page, one song after another (needs Spotify Premium). On iPhone, keep the screen on; if it has to be locked, use Play these in the Spotify app instead.

    Spotify or YouTube: the Spotify | YouTube switch picks where songs play from. YouTube plays full songs for everyone, no account needed, one after another; keep the screen on while it plays. Songs YouTube doesn't have yet are greyed out and get skipped. A few songs shared in the chat aren't on Spotify at all; they're marked YouTube only and play in YouTube mode.

    Spotify ↗: opens a single song in the Spotify app.

    Copy as playlist: copies the links for the songs on screen. On a computer, paste them into an empty playlist in the Spotify app and they all drop in.

    Tags: energy, mood and genre are judgement calls, not Spotify data. Songs marked ~ were harder to place.
